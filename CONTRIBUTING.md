# Contributing

Contributions that improve reproducibility, evidence quality, safety boundaries, or clarity are welcome.

## Useful contributions

Examples include:

- independent replications;
- controlled experiments, including null and contradictory results;
- corrections supported by primary sources;
- improved measurement or reporting methods;
- equipment-specific observations with complete operating details;
- datasets, analysis code, and uncertainty estimates;
- safety clarifications from qualified sources.

Commercial success, sensory preference, and mechanistic evidence should be reported as different kinds of claims.

## Experimental reporting

Experimental contributions should follow the repository's reproducibility standard and, where practical, include:

- a completed experiment record;
- raw measurements and units;
- actual temperature traces and measurement locations;
- coffee, water, grind, ratio, equipment, geometry, time, agitation, cooling, and filtration details;
- randomized sample codes and raw sensory responses;
- deviations, exclusions, failed runs, and alternative explanations;
- food-safety and equipment limitations.

Do not submit confidential client information, trade secrets belonging to another party, or personal information without authorization.
