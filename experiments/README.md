# Experiment Program

The experiment directory converts accumulated practical learning into traceable, reproducible evidence.

## Research sequence

Scientific priority and operational sequence are not identical.

### Foundational scientific experiment

- **P0 — Temperature response:** determine whether behavior changes continuously as extraction approaches nominally 121 °C or shows a repeatable nonlinear region. This requires suitable controlled-pressure equipment and direct temperature measurement. See [P0-TEMPERATURE-RESPONSE.md](../research/P0-TEMPERATURE-RESPONSE.md).

### First operational cycle

1. **P2 — Dilution response:** can begin immediately with an existing homogeneous concentrate.
2. **P1 — Hold time:** compare controlled hold conditions during production.
3. **P3 — Agitation:** compare the incremental effect of the established intentional procedure where this can be done safely.

See the [Research Roadmap](../research/RESEARCH-ROADMAP.md) for later work on matched-strength process comparison, water composition, clarification, resting, roast and ratio interactions, pressure and closure, and shelf life.

## Minimum design principles

- Change one primary variable at a time when possible.
- Record coffee, roast date, grinder, grind setting, batch size, vessel, water composition, and ratio convention.
- Define the outcome and decision rule before tasting.
- Randomize sample codes and presentation order for sensory comparisons.
- Include repeats and a reference condition.
- Record negative and null results.
- Separate instrumental observations, sensory responses, interpretations, and conclusions.
- Report actual measurement locations, methods, uncertainty, and deviations.
- Do not infer microbial safety from taste, smell, appearance, or process temperature alone.

Use [EXPERIMENT-TEMPLATE.md](EXPERIMENT-TEMPLATE.md) for new work.

## Suggested naming

Use a stable identifier such as:

`DB-YYYYMMDD-COFFEE-SEQ`

Example:

`DB-20260918-TARRAZU-01`

Samples derived from a batch can append conditions:

`DB-20260918-TARRAZU-01-DIL1plus5`

In records, define `DIL1plus5` explicitly—for example, one part concentrate plus five parts added water by mass.
